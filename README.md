# Corgi Platformer

A 2D platformer in Godot 4. You play a corgi who runs, jumps, dashes, and climbs walls while
hazards and a dirt cannon try to get her dirty. Get too dirty and the level restarts.

## Controls

| Move | Input |
|---|---|
| Run | A / D |
| Jump | Space |
| Wall jump | Space on a wall |
| Wall climb | Hold Space on a wall, W to climb (1.5s limit) |
| Dash | Shift (3 charges, 3s recharge each) |

## Status

In progress. Next: a bark that knocks dirt out of the air and scares enemies off, and a dirt gauge
in place of the health bar. Started in Godot 3.5 and ported to Godot 4.

## Running it

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7 and press Play.
